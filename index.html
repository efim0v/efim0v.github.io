About

Pinned · Mizzle Studio

Artem Efimov @efim0v

People need people.

In the age of AI it has become clear: neither intellectual nor physical work belongs to people alone anymore. Any big undertaking used to bring people together. Now you can do it on your own — you and a machine.

So now we reach out to each other not out of necessity but because we want to. And we are rediscovering conversation — not as a way to get something done, but as a value in itself.

I am co-founder and CEO of Mizzle Studio, an independent product studio built around this idea.

Open source

Small tools I made and share with the developer community. Free for anyone to use.

Brow

Swift · macOS

The 5-hour and weekly limits of every Claude Code account you have, in the MacBook notch, and when each one resets.

  • macOS 26 or later
  • Apple silicon
  • GPL-3.0

Website also in Русский and 简体中文

Grove

Swift · macOS

A menu-bar app and command-line tool that gives each feature a git worktree of every repository, all on one branch, and moves a session to another account when a limit runs out.

  • macOS 26 or later
  • Apple silicon
  • GPL-3.0
